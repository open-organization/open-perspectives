# Opening Up the Selling Process
This repository contains files related to *Open Up the Selling Process* by Ron McFarland, part of the [Open Perspectives series](https://github.com/open-organization/open-perspectives) from the Open Organization project.

## About the perspective
In *Opening Up the Selling Process*, Ron McFarland reflects on more than 30 years training and coaching salespeople and sales managers in the art of selling. He explores how open principles and practices, as well as recent advancements in customer relationship management technologies, have changed how we approach the selling process—and how they don't.

## About the author
Ron McFarland has been working in Japan for over 50 years, and he's spent more than 30 of them in international sales, sales management training, and expanding sales worldwide. He's worked in or been to more than 80 countries.
